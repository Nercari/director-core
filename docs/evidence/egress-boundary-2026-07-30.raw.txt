present, DNS dead; deny rule reshaped, DNS alive in RUN 3 — is the evidence that
a Block rule defeats an Allow rule at equal specificity on this build, and the
reason the final design carves DNS out of the Block rule by port range instead
of trying to permit it alongside one.
